The Engineering Narrative

Solving the Real Team Formation Crisis

In university engineering programs, project team formation is often left to chance or friendship cliques, causing severe skill gaps and friction. SkillSync turns team assembly into a fair, transparent, and data-driven process.

The Problem We Are Solving

Every semester, thousands of engineering students struggle with project group formation. Grouping exclusively by friendship circles or through ad-hoc WhatsApp messages routinely causes severe skill imbalances: teams end up with four frontend developers and no backend lead, or conflicting class schedules that stall development.

Our Engineering Solution

SkillSync eliminates the guesswork. Using deterministic candidate ranking, explicit skill requirement tagging (must-have vs. nice-to-have), availability matrix comparison, and live skill coverage analysis, students assemble well-rounded, capable teams equipped to deliver top-tier engineering coursework.
