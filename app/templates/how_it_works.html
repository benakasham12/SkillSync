Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Common questions from students building capstone and coursework projects on SkillSync.

How does deterministic matching work?

When a project lead sets must-have skills, SkillSync compares those against all match-ready students. The algorithm weights required skills (65%), schedule alignment (25%), and shared domains (10%) to output a transparent 0-100% score with clear breakdown tags.

Can students from different branches form a team?

Yes! SkillSync actively encourages interdisciplinary collaboration across CSE, ISE, ECE, AI&ML, Mechanical, and all BMSIT&M engineering branches.

When do contact details become visible?

To prevent unsolicited outreach and protect student privacy, phone numbers and personal contact information remain hidden until an invitation or application is formally accepted into the team.

How are project milestones tracked?

Each project workspace features an integrated sprint planner, member roster, dedicated real-time project group messaging, and attachment management.
