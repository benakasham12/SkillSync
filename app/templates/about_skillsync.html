The Background

Why We Replaced Ad-Hoc Group Formation

In typical university settings, project teams form haphazardly within immediate social circles or ad-hoc messaging chats. This routinely produces imbalanced teams: multiple frontend developers with no backend engineer, or students with conflicting lab timetables that prevent meetings.

SkillSync solves this problem at the root. By parsing explicit skill requirements (must-have vs. nice-to-have), comparing weekly free slots, and evaluating shared domain interests, teams form around genuine technical and temporal synergy.

The Philosophy

Deterministic, Merit-Driven Alignment

SkillSync operates with zero algorithmic black boxes. Every match score is computed transparently: 65% based on skill overlap, 25% on weekly time slot alignment, and 10% on shared domain category interest.

Students see exactly why they matched, what skills they bring to the team, and where coverage gaps still exist, empowering informed team decisions.
